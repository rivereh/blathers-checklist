Blathers' Checklist

A tracking app for Animal Crossing: New Horizons

What is this?

A checklist for Animal Crossing: New Horizons that shows the fish, bugs and sea creatures you can catch right now and still haven't caught yet.

How to use it

  • Pick a month to see what's around. It starts on the current month.
  • Turn on Available this hour to only show what you can catch right now.
  • Turn on Southern Hemisphere if your island is in the south.
  • Switch between Fish, Bugs and Sea Creatures with the tabs, and use the dropdowns to sort or filter by location.
  • Hit ✓ on anything you've caught to move it to the Caught list at the bottom. Click the ✕ there to undo.

Your caught list and hemisphere are saved in this browser.
